# TinkerTime Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 September 2026**

TinkerTime is designed so that there is almost nothing to write here. There is
no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server that stores your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

We do not collect, transmit, sell or store any personal information. There is
no TinkerTime server for your data to be sent to.

## What stays on your device

The following is saved on your phone and never leaves it:

- Your children's names or nicknames, and their ages
- The toys you add to your toy shelf
- Activities you save or have played
- Your app settings

This information is stored only in the app's own private storage. Uninstalling
TinkerTime deletes all of it permanently. You can export it yourself at any
time from **Settings → Backup and restore**, which writes a plain, readable
file that you control and that we never see.

## Children's privacy

TinkerTime is designed for parents and carers to use, not for children to use
alone. A child's name and age are stored solely to pick age-appropriate
activities, they stay on the device, and they are never transmitted anywhere.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children under 13. There is no mechanism in the app by which we could.

## The only two outbound connections

In the interests of being complete rather than merely reassuring, these are the
only times TinkerTime contacts the internet at all:

**1. Checking for content updates.** When you open the app it asks Expo's
update service whether new activities are available. This request carries your
app version and device platform, as any app update check does. It carries
nothing about you or your children. Expo's privacy policy is at
<https://expo.dev/privacy>.

**2. When you tap a shopping link.** If you tap a link to buy a toy, your
device's browser opens Amazon. From that point you are on Amazon's website and
their privacy policy applies, not ours. We never send Amazon anything about
you, your children or your toy shelf — the link is a plain product search.

The app works completely offline apart from these two things. Every activity
and every toy is stored inside the app itself.

## Affiliate links

TinkerTime is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an
affiliate advertising programme. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying
purchases.

This costs you nothing extra, and it does not change which activities you are
shown. Toy suggestions are ranked purely by how many activities each one would
unlock for your child's age — a number the app calculates — not by what pays
the most commission.

## Purchases

If TinkerTime Pro is available and you buy it, the purchase is handled entirely
by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment
details. We only receive confirmation of whether a purchase is active.

## Analytics and advertising

There are none. TinkerTime contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting that
transmits personal data, no advertising network and no tracking of any kind. We
do not know how many activities you have done, which ones, or when.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct
or delete. Your data is in your hands: uninstalling the app removes everything,
and the backup file is yours alone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date.
Material changes will also be noted in the app.
